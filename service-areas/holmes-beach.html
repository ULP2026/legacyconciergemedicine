Care in Holmes Beach

Personal medicine for island residents

Holmes Beach is the central and most residential of Anna Maria Island's three cities, a place of quiet streets, canal-front homes, and easy beach access. Families and retirees settle here for genuine island life close to the Gulf.

Legacy makes that life simpler. With a physician who comes to your Holmes Beach home, there is no crossing the bridge for routine care, just unhurried, personal medicine with direct access whenever you need it.

Holmes Beach Questions

Frequently asked in Holmes Beach.

Do you serve homes throughout Holmes Beach?

Yes. We make in-home visits across Holmes Beach, from Key Royale to the canal neighborhoods, all within the 34217 ZIP code.

On-island options are limited, which is why members value a physician who comes to them rather than crossing the bridge to Bradenton.

Can you care for both seasonal and year-round residents?

Yes. We keep your history continuous, so care is seamless whether you live here full-time or seasonally.

Members have direct phone and message access to their care team, so you always reach someone who knows you.
