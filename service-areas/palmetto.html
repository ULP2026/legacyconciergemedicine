Care in Palmetto

Steady care for an established community

Palmetto sits just across the river from Bradenton, with a working waterfront, the greenery of Emerson Point, and the quiet of Terra Ceia nearby. It is an established community where many residents have lived for decades.

Legacy brings care that honors that stability, a physician who comes to your Palmetto home, knows your history, and stays with you over the long term, with the time and attention conventional clinics rarely offer.

Palmetto Questions

Frequently asked in Palmetto.

Does your service area include Palmetto?

Yes. Palmetto (34221) is part of our Manatee County coverage, including Riverside, Terra Ceia, and Snead Island.

Do you make house calls in the older parts of Palmetto?

We do. In-home visits reach every part of Palmetto, from historic downtown to the waterfront neighborhoods.

I have lived here for decades. Can you coordinate my existing care?

Yes. We take the time to understand your full history and coordinate with the specialists you already know and trust.

What makes concierge care different from my current doctor?

A limited patient panel means your physician has time to truly know you, comes to your home, and is directly reachable between visits.
