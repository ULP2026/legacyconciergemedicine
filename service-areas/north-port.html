Care in North Port

Personal care in a spread-out city

North Port covers a lot of ground, from the historic waters of Warm Mineral Springs to the newer neighborhoods rising near Wellen Park. It is a spread-out, growing community of both families and retirees, where clinics can be crowded and far between.

Legacy answers that with care that comes to you. Instead of long drives and packed waiting rooms, North Port members get a dedicated physician at home, with the time and access that make medicine feel personal again.
