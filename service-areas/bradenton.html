Care in Bradenton

Rooted care for a city with roots

Bradenton is the heart of Manatee County, a city with real roots, from the downtown Riverwalk and the Village of the Arts to the shaded streets of West Bradenton and the fishing heritage of nearby Cortez. It is a community of families and long-time residents.

Legacy brings personal medicine into that community. With in-home concierge care, Bradenton residents get a physician who knows them, coordinates with local specialists, and stays reachable, without the wait that defines conventional clinics.

Bradenton Questions

Frequently asked in Bradenton.

Which areas of Bradenton do you serve?

We provide in-home care across Bradenton, including downtown, West Bradenton, Palma Sola, and Bayshore Gardens, covering the 34205 through 34210 ZIP codes.

Can you coordinate with Manatee County specialists and hospitals?

Yes. We keep your specialists and any hospital care working from one plan, so your care stays connected across the county.

Do you serve both families and older adults in Bradenton?

We do. Our practice cares for adults and families, with particular strength in primary and geriatric medicine.

How do I reach my physician between visits?

Members have direct phone and message access to their care team, so you always reach someone who knows you.
