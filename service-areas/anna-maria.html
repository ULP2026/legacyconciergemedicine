Care in Anna Maria

Island care without crossing the bridge

Anna Maria guards its old-Florida character, from the cottages and Pine Avenue shops at the north tip of the island to Bean Point, where the Gulf meets Tampa Bay. There are no high-rises here, and on-island medical care is limited.

Legacy bridges that distance. Rather than crossing the bridge into Bradenton for routine care, Anna Maria residents have their physician come to them, with the continuity and direct access that island living quietly requires.

Anna Maria Questions

Frequently asked in Anna Maria.

Why is concierge care valuable in Anna Maria?

On-island medical options are limited and the island is connected by bridge. Having your physician come to you removes long trips for routine and follow-up care.

Which ZIP code does Anna Maria fall under?

The city of Anna Maria is 34216, at the north end of the island, well within our Manatee County coverage.

I am here only part of the year. Can I join?

Yes. Many island members are seasonal. We hold your history and plan year-round so care picks up where it left off.

Can you coordinate mainland care when needed?

Yes. We arrange labs, imaging, and specialists in Bradenton and beyond, and fold everything into your plan.
