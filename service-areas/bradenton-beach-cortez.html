Care in Bradenton Beach & Cortez

Personal care for two waterfront communities

Few places hold onto old Florida like these do. Cortez remains one of the last working fishing villages on the Gulf, just across the water from Bradenton Beach at the south end of Anna Maria Island, with its cottages, the Historic Bridge Street Pier, and a pace set by the tides.

Legacy fits that character. We bring a physician directly to homes in Cortez and Bradenton Beach, so island and waterfront living never means a long drive for care, just unhurried, personal medicine with direct access when you need it.

Bradenton Beach & Cortez Questions

Frequently asked in Bradenton Beach & Cortez.

Do you serve both Cortez and Bradenton Beach?

Yes. We make in-home visits to the historic Cortez fishing village and throughout Bradenton Beach on the south end of Anna Maria Island.

What ZIP codes cover these communities?

Bradenton Beach falls under 34217 and the Cortez area under 34215. Both are within our Manatee County coverage.

Is concierge care practical on the island?

Very much so. With limited local medical options, having your physician come to you removes long trips off the island for routine care.

Can you care for seasonal residents here?

Yes. We keep your records continuous year to year, so care resumes seamlessly whenever you return.
