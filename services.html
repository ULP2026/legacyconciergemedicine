Concierge primary care

What your care includes.

Legacy Concierge Medicine provides concierge primary care for adults and families, delivered almost entirely in the comfort of your home. All routine visits, follow-ups, and care planning are conducted in-home whenever possible.

For patients requiring a higher level of care, including those in skilled nursing settings, care continues seamlessly within those environments — preserving trust and continuity.

  • Private, in-home visits
    Routine care, follow-ups, and care planning where you live — so we understand the real context of your life.
  • Unrushed appointments
    Longer visits that uncover more than a rushed physical ever could.
  • Direct access to your physician
    A relationship with the people who know your history, not a system that hands you off.
  • Preventive & longevity-focused strategy
    Personalized, sustainable medical plans built around your lifestyle, environment, and goals.
  • Thoughtful medication management
    Meticulous review of your history, removing what is unnecessary so every plan is as personal as you are.
  • Continuity through life's transitions
    The same care team through independent living, assisted living, and skilled nursing — anticipating change before crises occur.
  • Care for the whole family
    Aging parents, adult children, and everyone planning ahead — one physician who knows the whole story.

Common questions

What to expect.

Is concierge medicine only for the ultra-wealthy?

No. Concierge medicine isn't about luxury — it's about access. Our patients are professionals, caregivers, aging adults, and families planning ahead who feel underserved by rushed, traditional healthcare and want a true relationship with their physician.

What happens on a private consultation?

A private consultation is a calm, unhurried conversation about your health, your goals, and what you're looking for in a physician. We'll explain how the practice works, answer your questions, and discuss membership details so you can decide whether Legacy is the right fit.

What does the first visit look like?

Your first visit takes place in your home whenever possible. Before we ever talk about your chart, we take the time to understand the real context of your life — your history, your family, and the life you're working to protect — and begin building a personalized, sustainable plan.

What if my needs change over time?

Continuity is a defining feature of the practice. If safety or health requires a transition into assisted living or skilled nursing, your care continues within that environment with the same physician and nurse practitioner team. You are never handed off to strangers.

Do you care for seasonal residents?

Yes. Whether you're in Southwest Florida half the year or all of it, your care stays continuous. Ask about how we support part-time residents during your consultation.
