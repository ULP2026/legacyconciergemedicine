Common questions

The questions members ask first.

What is concierge primary care?

It is a membership-based model where a physician intentionally limits how many patients they care for. That smaller panel gives you generous time, direct access, and a doctor who truly knows you.

Do you really provide care at home?

Yes. Whenever it is appropriate, your care is delivered in the comfort of your home. Virtual visits are available when they serve you better, and a clinic setting when needed.

How does membership work?

You begin with a conversation, we make sure the practice is the right fit, and then you enroll. From there, your care is continuous, relationship-based, and directly accessible.

Does membership replace my insurance?

No. A concierge membership covers the primary care relationship; it is not insurance and does not replace it. We are glad to explain how it works alongside your existing coverage.
